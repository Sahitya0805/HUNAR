HUNAR (हुनर)

Autonomous Voice-Driven Rural RPL Certification & Spatial GIA Livelihood Mapping Engine

An affirmative digital public infrastructure engineered to convert informal rural trade mastery into official National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) certifications within 1-day commutable proximities.

Target Workforce 150M+ Rural Artisans
Primary Pathway Direct RPL (12–16 Hrs)
Commute Bound ≤ 25 km (1-Day Return)
Per-Query Cost ₹0 (Self-Hosted AI)

1. Problem Context: The Rural Certification Paradox

India's rural economy is powered by millions of masons, agro-millers, solar technicians, carpenters, and artisans who have accumulated 5 to 15+ years of daily practical mastery. However, over 92% possess zero formal certification, locking them out of institutional bank credit, government procurement (GeM), and wage parity.

Why Conventional Skilling Schemes Fail:

Existing schemes mandate 3 to 6-month classroom courses at distant urban ITIs. For a daily-wage rural earner, attending these courses means immediate forfeiture of daily income (₹400–600/day). Consequently, drop-out rates exceed 70%, and PM-AJAY GIA funds remain underutilized.

2. The Four Architectural Non-Negotiables

• RPL-First Fast-Tracking

Certifies existing experience in 12–16 Hours (2 Days) instead of redundant 6-month classroom enrollment.

• Strict 1-Day Commute Limit

Only recommends centers within ≤ 25 km, ensuring artisans return home the same evening without wage loss.

• Deterministic NQR Retrieval

Speech is normalized for dialect; all QP-NOS codes and syllabi are retrieved deterministically from NCVET registers.

• Zero Per-Query Cloud Cost

Runs offline-first on self-hosted AI4Bharat IndicASR models with zero recurring commercial API paywalls.

3. End-to-End Processing Architecture

Hunar implements an air-gapped, modular processing pipeline designed for low-connectivity rural Common Service Centers (CSCs):

1
Acoustic Voice Ingestion: Web Audio API capturing native dialect speech (Bundelkhandi, Bhojpuri, Marathi, etc.).
2
Entity & Experience Extraction: Multilingual regex tokenizers parsing years of practice (e.g., "7 साल"), tools, and materials.
3
Deterministic NQR Matcher: Weighted semantic TF-IDF scoring against official NCVET qualification registers.
4
GIS Commute Verification: Haversine router validating road distance against $\le 25\text{ km}$ 1-day threshold.

4. Mathematical Reachability & Commute Equation

Reachability Rule:
$$\text{Reachable}(\text{Artisan}, \text{Center}_i) = \begin{cases} \text{TRUE (Direct RPL Camp)} & \text{if } \text{Dist}(\text{Artisan}, \text{Center}_i) \le 25\text{ km} \\ \text{FALSE (GIA Mobile Van Void)} & \text{if } \text{Dist}(\text{Artisan}, \text{Center}_i) > 25\text{ km} \end{cases}$$
