the rural certification paradox

A 6-month course costs a daily-wage earner ₹54,000 they do not have.

Classroom schemes ask experienced artisans to stop earning in order to prove what they already know. HUNAR certifies the skill they have, where they live.

how it works

Four steps. No forms, no typing.

01

Speak

The artisan describes their work at the village CSC kiosk in Hindi, Bundelkhandi, Marathi, Tamil or Telugu.

02

Match

A deterministic engine scores the narrative against the register and shows exactly which words matched.

03

Route

Only centres inside a one-day commute are recommended. Anything further is flagged as a reachability void.

04

Sync

Every intake is saved offline, then replicated to all kiosks in the block. Voids add up into a fundable van proposal.
